01 / CONTENT & GROWTH

An independent
content experiment.

Personal project

The idea

Build an Instagram presence from the ground up using AI-assisted content production, short-form video and consistent experimentation. I handle ideation, creative production, captions, publishing and audience engagement.

1.6M+organic views in approximately 48 hours
189Ktop Reel views in about 48 hours
2,496+organic followers
500+net followers added in approximately 48 hours

My approach

  • Plan and edit Reels using Instagram Edits, Canva and AI-assisted tools.
  • Write SEO-focused captions and manage audience interactions.
  • Use Instagram Insights to understand reach, engagement and response.
  • Run A/B experiments with formats, captions and posting times to refine content.
  • Connect ideation, AI image generation, captioning and publishing in a repeatable content workflow.

Tools

ChatGPT, AI image generation, Canva, Instagram Edits and Instagram Insights.

Results reflect an organic growth period without paid advertising. Account identity is kept private.
